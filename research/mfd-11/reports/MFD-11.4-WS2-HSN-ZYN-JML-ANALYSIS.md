## 4. Evidence For/Against Independent Aesthetic Category

### 4.1 Evidence AGAINST Independent Aesthetic Category

1. **No Quranic verse predicates beauty as an attribute of Allah using any of these three roots.** The statement "Allah is beautiful and loves beauty" (inna Allaha jamīlun yuḥibbu al-jamāl) is hadith (Sahih Muslim 91a), NOT Quranic text.

2. **ح-س-ن is overwhelmingly moral/ethical (81%).** The Form IV *aḥsana* consistently means "perfected/made excellent" in contexts of divine creation, moral conduct, or eschatological reward — never artistic beautification.

3. **ز-ي-ن is predominantly negative (67% deception/test).** When positive, it describes either divine cosmic arrangement (creation-signs) or faith made pleasing in hearts (moral psychology). Personal adornment usage is legal regulation.

4. **ج-م-ل occurs exactly 3 times (+1 compound).** Two modify *ṣabr* (patience), one describes livestock. No abstract aesthetic discourse exists.

5. **All borderline-aesthetic verses overlap with existing domains:**
   - 32:7, 40:64, 64:3, 95:4 → creation-signs (divine perfection/proportion)
   - 15:16, 37:6, 41:12, 50:6, 67:5 → creation-signs (cosmic signs)
   - 16:6 → provision/livestock benefits
   - 12:18, 12:83 → ethics/patience

6. **No morphological form across these roots produces a dedicated "beauty" or "art" noun.** *Jamāl* (the closest candidate) occurs once and refers to livestock. *Zīnah* means adornment/decoration, overwhelmingly in negative or legal contexts. *Ḥusn* means goodness/excellence, overwhelmingly moral.

### 4.2 Evidence FOR Retaining Minimal Aesthetic Reference

1. **16:6 (*jamālun*)** genuinely describes sensory/aesthetic pleasure derived from observing livestock. This is the sole unambiguous aesthetic reference across all three roots.

2. **The 5 cosmic adornment verses (15:16, 37:6, 41:12, 50:6, 67:5)** use *zayyana* positively to describe divine arrangement of the heavens. While functioning as creation-signs, they acknowledge visual splendor as part of the sign-function.

3. **49:7** uses *zayyana* positively for faith made pleasing in hearts — aesthetic language applied to spiritual experience.

**Assessment:** These do NOT constitute an independent aesthetic domain. They are subordinate elements within creation-signs, ethics, and provision topics. Retaining them as a standalone "arts-beauty-aesthetics" domain would be a category error.
