### Diagnosis

**MAPPING GAP + CONCEPTUAL CONFLATION.**

This is NOT a pure extraction gap. The Qur'anic material relevant to aesthetics and beauty IS extensively extracted across 217 matches in 40+ files. However, ZERO candidates map to `husn-concept`, making this a severe mapping gap.

More critically, there is a **conceptual conflation problem** in the topic definition itself:

1. **The ḥ-s-n root does NOT primarily mean aesthetic beauty in the Qur'an.** Of the ~140 ḥ-s-n matches in extractions, approximately 85% refer to:
   - *Ihsan* as moral excellence/kindness (to parents, in divorce, in social conduct)
   - *Qard hasan* as goodly loan
   - *Husn al-ma'ab* as best return/reward
   - *Ahsana* as perfection/completion of creation (functional excellence, not visual beauty)

2. **Genuine aesthetic content uses DIFFERENT roots:**
   - *Jamāl* (جمال): explicit aesthetic beauty — 16:6 (livestock beauty)
   - *Zīnah/Zayyana* (زينة/زيّن): adornment/decoration — 15:16, 18:7, 37:6, 67:5, 3:14, 57:20
   - *Bahjah* (بهجة): splendor/radiance — minimal extraction presence
   - *Ḥilyah* (حلية): ornamentation — not found in extractions

3. **The borderline cases (32:7, 95:4, 64:3, 40:64, 82:7)** use *ahsana/sawwā/'adala* to describe divine perfection of creation. Whether this constitutes "beauty" or "functional excellence" is a legitimate scholarly debate. Classical tafsir traditions differ: some read *ahsana* as aesthetic perfection, others as teleological/functional perfection.

4. **Eschatological beauty passages (55:58-76, 76:19, 83:22-28)** describe Jannah's aesthetic qualities using *khayrāt ḥisān*, *ḥūr maqṣūrāt*, *lū'lū' manthūr* — these ARE genuinely aesthetic but are currently unmapped to any beauty topic.

5. **The domain `arts-beauty-aesthetics` has only 2 topics** (`husn-concept` and `art-boundaries`), both with zero candidate coverage. This suggests the entire domain was scaffolded taxonomically before extraction/mapping work addressed it.
