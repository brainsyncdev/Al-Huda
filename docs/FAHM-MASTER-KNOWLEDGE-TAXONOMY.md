# FAHM Master Knowledge Taxonomy

## Overview

The FAHM Master Knowledge Taxonomy is the canonical knowledge structure for the Quran Al Huda project, organizing Islamic knowledge across all major domains of human life.

**Total Statistics:**
- **Domains:** 20 (14 existing + 6 proposed from audit)
- **Dimensions:** 108 (100 seed + 8 proposed additions)
- **Subdimensions:** 25 (meaningful groupings)
- **Topics:** 150+ (consolidated from 480+ research findings)
- **Relationships:** 150+ (hierarchical + cross-domain + thematic)

## Cross-Domain Relationship Map

### 10 Cross-Cutting Themes

1. **Balance & Moderation (Mizan/Wasat)** — Ethics, worship, economics, environment
2. **Trust & Responsibility (Amanah)** — Wealth, body, environment, leadership
3. **Signs & Reflection (Ayat/Tafakkur)** — Creation, scripture, history, self
4. **Gratitude & Ingratitude (Shukr/Kufr al-Ni'mah)** — Blessings, wealth, health
5. **Trial & Test (Bala'/Fitnah)** — Wealth, children, hardship, blessings
6. **Justice & Oppression ('Adl/Zulm)** — Social, economic, family, governance
7. **Remembrance & Forgetfulness (Dhikr/Ghaflah)** — God, covenant, hereafter
8. **Patience & Haste (Sabr/'Ajalah)** — Hardship, worship, sin, judgment
9. **Knowledge & Ignorance ('Ilm/Jahl)** — Divine vs. human knowledge
10. **Covenant & Breach ('Ahd/Wafa'/Nakth)** — Divine, prophetic, marital, treaties

### Key Cross-Domain Relationships

- **Tawheed ↔ Worship**: Divine unity underpins all worship
- **Prophets ↔ Ethics**: Prophetic character as moral model
- **Quran ↔ Law**: Divine revelation as legal source
- **Family ↔ Society**: Family as building block of society
- **Economics ↔ Ethics**: Business conduct governed by moral principles
- **Environment ↔ Stewardship**: Creation care as divine trust
- **Governance ↔ Justice**: Political authority bound by justice
- **Health ↔ Body**: Physical wellbeing as divine trust
- **War ↔ Peace**: Conflict resolution through ethical means
- **Arts ↔ Beauty**: Creative expression within Islamic boundaries
