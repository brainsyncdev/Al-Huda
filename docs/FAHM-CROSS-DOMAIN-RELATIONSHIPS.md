# FAHM Cross-Domain Relationships

## Overview

This document maps the cross-cutting themes and relationships that span multiple domains in the FAHM taxonomy. These relationships demonstrate the interconnected nature of Islamic knowledge.

---

## 10 Cross-Cutting Themes

### 1. Balance & Moderation (Mizan/Wasat)
**Spans:** Ethics, Worship, Economics, Environment

**Key Topics:**
- balance-moderation (ethics-character)
- mizan-balance (creation-signs, environment-stewardship)
- moderation in spending (economic-conduct)
- moderation in worship (worship-practice)

**Relationship Pattern:** The principle of balance appears across all life domains, from personal ethics to environmental stewardship.

---

### 2. Trust & Responsibility (Amanah)
**Spans:** Wealth, Body, Environment, Leadership

**Key Topics:**
- trust-responsibility (ethics-character)
- khalifah-stewardship (environment-stewardship)
- body-as-trust (health-medicine)
- wealth-ownership (economic-conduct)
- leadership-authority (governance-political-authority)

**Relationship Pattern:** Everything—wealth, body, environment, authority—is a trust from Allah requiring accountability.

---

### 3. Signs & Reflection (Ayat/Tafakkur)
**Spans:** Creation, Scripture, History, Self

**Key Topics:**
- signs-and-reflection (human-experiences)
- reflection-tafakkur (human-experiences)
- animals-as-communities (creation-signs)
- natural-resources (creation-signs)
- quranic-linguistics (revelation-guidance)
- prophetic-lessons (prophets-narratives)

**Relationship Pattern:** Divine signs appear in creation, scripture, history, and self—inviting reflection in all domains.

---

### 4. Gratitude & Ingratitude (Shukr/Kufr al-Ni'mah)
**Spans:** Blessings, Wealth, Health

**Key Topics:**
- gratitude-ingratitude (ethics-character)
- shukr-gratitude (ethics-character)
- wealth-distribution (economic-conduct)
- prophetic-medicine (health-medicine)
- divine-mercy (belief-theology)

**Relationship Pattern:** Gratitude for blessings (wealth, health, faith) is a core virtue; ingratitude is a major vice.

---

### 5. Trial & Test (Bala'/Fitnah)
**Spans:** Wealth, Children, Hardship, Blessings

**Key Topics:**
- trial-and-test (human-experiences)
- sabr-patience (ethics-character)
- poverty-solutions (society-justice)
- wealth-distribution (economic-conduct)
- child-development (family-relationships)

**Relationship Pattern:** Life is a test through both hardship and blessings; patience and gratitude are the responses.

---

### 6. Justice & Oppression ('Adl/Zulm)
**Spans:** Social, Economic, Family, Governance

**Key Topics:**
- justice-oppression (society-justice)
- human-equality (society-justice)
- economic-justice (economic-conduct)
- marital-rights (family-relationships)
- leadership-authority (governance-political-authority)
- criminal-law (law-obligations)

**Relationship Pattern:** Justice is a universal principle applying to all human interactions; oppression is prohibited in all forms.

---

### 7. Remembrance & Forgetfulness (Dhikr/Ghaflah)
**Spans:** God, Covenant, Hereafter

**Key Topics:**
- remembrance-forgetfulness (worship-practice)
- dua-etiquette (worship-practice)
- night-prayer (worship-practice)
- covenant-breach (law-obligations)
- resurrection-process (eschatology-afterlife)

**Relationship Pattern:** Remembering Allah, covenants, and the hereafter is essential; forgetfulness leads to spiritual decline.

---

### 8. Patience & Haste (Sabr/'Ajalah)
**Spans:** Hardship, Worship, Sin, Judgment

**Key Topics:**
- patience-haste (ethics-character)
- sabr-patience (ethics-character)
- ramadan-fasting (worship-practice)
- repentance-conditions (ethics-character)
- day-of-judgment (eschatology-afterlife)

**Relationship Pattern:** Patience is required in worship, hardship, and avoiding sin; haste is condemned in all contexts.

---

### 9. Knowledge & Ignorance ('Ilm/Jahl)
**Spans:** Divine vs. Human Knowledge

**Key Topics:**
- knowledge-ignorance (human-experiences)
- knowledge-seeking (human-experiences)
- intellect-importance (human-experiences)
- divine-knowledge (belief-theology)
- quranic-sciences (revelation-guidance)

**Relationship Pattern:** Knowledge is highly valued; ignorance is the root of many problems. Divine knowledge is infinite.

---

### 10. Covenant & Breach ('Ahd/Wafa'/Nakth)
**Spans:** Divine, Prophetic, Marital, Treaties

**Key Topics:**
- covenant-breach (law-obligations)
- covenants-ahd (law-obligations)
- marriage-contract (family-relationships)
- peace-treaties (war-peace-conflict)
- pillars-of-iman (belief-theology)

**Relationship Pattern:** Covenants—whether with Allah, spouses, or nations—must be honored; breach is a serious sin.

## Examples of Topics Spanning Multiple Domains

### Example 1: Tawheed (Divine Unity)
- **Primary Domain:** Belief & Theology
- **Cross-Domain Connections:**
  - Worship Practice (all worship directed to Allah alone)
  - Prophets & Narratives (all prophets called to tawheed)
  - Ethics & Character (sincerity in worship)
  - Eschatology (shirk as unforgivable sin)

### Example 2: Salah (Prayer)
- **Primary Domain:** Worship Practice
- **Cross-Domain Connections:**
  - Belief & Theology (manifestation of faith)
  - Ethics & Character (discipline, sincerity)
  - Society & Justice (congregational prayer, brotherhood)
  - Health & Medicine (physical movements, mental peace)

### Example 3: Marriage
- **Primary Domain:** Family & Relationships
- **Cross-Domain Connections:**
  - Ethics & Character (love, mercy, patience)
  - Law & Obligations (marital rights, divorce)
  - Economic Conduct (mahr, maintenance)
  - Society & Justice (family as social unit)

### Example 4: Business Ethics
- **Primary Domain:** Economic Conduct
- **Cross-Domain Connections:**
  - Ethics & Character (honesty, fairness)
  - Law & Obligations (contracts, prohibitions)
  - Society & Justice (economic justice, poverty)
  - Worship Practice (halal income as worship)

### Example 5: Environmental Stewardship
- **Primary Domain:** Environment & Stewardship
- **Cross-Domain Connections:**
  - Belief & Theology (khalifah concept)
  - Ethics & Character (responsibility, moderation)
  - Creation & Signs (mizan balance)
  - Economic Conduct (sustainable economics)

## Relationship Type Definitions

### CONTAINS
**Definition:** Hierarchical parent-child relationship
**Example:** Domain "Belief & Theology" CONTAINS dimension "Allah & Divine Attributes"
**Count:** 100 relationships

### BELONGS_TO
**Definition:** Inverse of CONTAINS (child to parent)
**Example:** Dimension "Allah & Divine Attributes" BELONGS_TO domain "Belief & Theology"
**Count:** Implicit in CONTAINS relationships

### RELATED_TO
**Definition:** Semantic association between topics
**Example:** Topic "Tawheed" RELATED_TO topic "Shirk Prohibition"
**Count:** Embedded in relatedTopicIds arrays

### CROSS_REFERENCES
**Definition:** Explicit cross-domain topic connections
**Example:** Topic "Adam Story" CROSS_REFERENCES topic "Human Creation"
**Count:** 40+ relationships

### SHARES_CONCEPT
**Definition:** Topics sharing a cross-cutting theme
**Example:** Topic "Balance & Moderation" SHARES_CONCEPT with topic "Mizan Balance"
**Count:** 10 relationships (one per cross-cutting theme)

## Navigation Patterns

### Pattern 1: Theme-Based Navigation
Users can explore knowledge by cross-cutting themes:
- Start with theme (e.g., "Balance & Moderation")
- See all related topics across domains
- Navigate to specific domain contexts

### Pattern 2: Domain-Based Navigation
Users can explore within a domain:
- Start with domain (e.g., "Economic Conduct")
- See dimensions and topics
- Follow cross-references to other domains

### Pattern 3: Topic-Based Navigation
Users can explore from a specific topic:
- Start with topic (e.g., "Marriage")
- See related topics via relatedTopicIds
- Follow cross-domain relationships
- Explore cross-cutting themes
