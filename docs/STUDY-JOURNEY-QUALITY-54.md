## Unresolved Editorial Issues

### 1. Generic Completion Criteria

**Severity:** Warning  
**Affected:** All 4 journeys  
**Description:** All journeys include generic completion criteria such as:
- "Completed all 5 stages"
- "Reflected on each ayah's meaning"

These are process-oriented rather than outcome-oriented.

**Recommendation:** Replace with specific, measurable outcomes. For example:
- Instead of "Completed all 5 stages" → "Can explain the four verses of Surah Al-Ikhlas and their significance"
- Instead of "Reflected on each ayah's meaning" → "Can articulate personal insights from each stage's reflection"

**Action Required:** Editorial review to rewrite completion criteria

### 2. Topic Diversity in Gratitude Journey

**Severity:** Warning  
**Affected:** Journey 3 (Gratitude and Contentment)  
**Description:** All 5 stages reference only the "gratitude" topic. While thematically consistent, this limits exposure to related concepts.

**Recommendation:** Consider incorporating related topics:
- Stage 2: Could reference "remembrance" (already linked in topic relations)
- Stage 4: Could reference "knowledge-and-reflection"

**Action Required:** Editorial decision on topic diversity vs. thematic focus

### 3. Ayah Reference Reuse

**Severity:** Informational  
**Affected:** Journey 4 Stage 1  
**Description:** Stage 1 of "Building a Relationship with Allah" uses ayah 112:1, which is also used in Stage 1 of "Understanding Tawheed".

**Assessment:** This is acceptable because:
- Journey 4 has Journey 1 as a prerequisite
- The objective is different (foundation for relationship vs. understanding tawheed)
- The reflection prompt is different

**Action Required:** None - acceptable reuse with clear pedagogical purpose
