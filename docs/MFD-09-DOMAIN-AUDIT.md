## Cross-Domain Issues

### Overlap and Boundaries

1. **belief-theology ↔ revelation-guidance**: Some overlap in divine attributes and revelation concepts
2. **ethics-character ↔ society-justice**: Moral virtues vs. social responsibility boundaries need clarification
3. **economic-conduct ↔ law-obligations**: Commercial law overlaps between domains
4. **human-experiences ↔ health-medicine**: Psychology and mental wellbeing span both domains
5. **environment-stewardship ↔ creation-signs**: Natural world topics could belong to either

### Missing Cross-Domain Connections

1. **Technology and Ethics**: digital-technology-ethics is in society-justice but impacts all domains
2. **Contemporary Applications**: Many domains lack modern context topics
3. **Comparative Studies**: Limited comparative Islamic studies topics
4. **Historical Development**: Missing historical development of concepts across domains

## Conclusion

The domain structure is conceptually sound but severely imbalanced in topic distribution. Core theological and ethical domains are well-covered, while specialized domains representing important aspects of Islamic knowledge are critically underrepresented. Immediate priority should be given to expanding creation-signs, interfaith-communities, language-vocabulary, health-medicine, arts-beauty-aesthetics, and work-vocation domains.

The taxonomy needs approximately 150-200 additional topics to achieve balanced coverage across all 20 domains, with particular emphasis on the 6 critically underrepresented domains that currently have only 1-2 topics each.
