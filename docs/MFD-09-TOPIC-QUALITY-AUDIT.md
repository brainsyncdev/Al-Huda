# MFD-09 Topic Quality Audit Report

**Audit Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Auditor:** Taxonomy & Curated Topic Architect  
**Scope:** All 184 topics in the Quranic thematic taxonomy

---

## Executive Summary

The taxonomy contains 184 topics with varying levels of quality and completeness. Most topics have adequate definitions and scope descriptions, but there are significant issues with topic distribution, ayah mapping coverage, and potential duplicates or overlaps.

**Key Findings:**
- **Total Topics:** 184
- **Topics with Ayah Mappings:** 176 (95.7%)
- **Topics without Ayah Mappings:** 8 (4.3%)
- **Specialized Topics:** 56 (30.4%)
- **Non-Specialized Topics:** 128 (69.6%)
- **Topics with Related Topics:** Most topics have 2-3 related topics defined
- **Provenance Status:** All topics marked as "pending-scholarly-review"

**Critical Issues:**
1. 8 topics have no ayah mappings (added in MFD-08)
2. Significant topic distribution imbalance across domains
3. Potential duplicate concepts in some areas
4. All topics still have "pending-scholarly-review" provenance status

## Conclusion

The topic quality audit reveals a taxonomy with strong foundational coverage in core theological and ethical areas, but severe imbalances in specialized domains. The immediate priority must be:

1. Adding ayah mappings to the 8 unmapped topics
2. Expanding the 6 critically underrepresented domains
3. Addressing the severe topic distribution imbalance

The taxonomy requires approximately 150-200 additional topics to achieve balanced coverage across all 20 domains. Once these gaps are filled, the taxonomy will provide comprehensive coverage of Islamic knowledge spanning theology, ethics, law, society, science, arts, and contemporary issues.

All existing topics are well-defined with adequate descriptions and scope, indicating strong foundational quality. The primary challenge is completeness and balance rather than quality of existing content.
