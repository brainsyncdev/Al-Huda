# MFD-09 Missing Topics Report

**Audit Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Auditor:** Taxonomy & Curated Topic Architect  
**Scope:** Proposed new topics to fill gaps across all 20 domains

---

## Executive Summary

The taxonomy has 184 topics distributed across 20 domains and 103 dimensions. Analysis reveals:
- **18 dimensions with 0 topics** (critical gaps)
- **48 dimensions with only 1 topic** (severely underrepresented)
- **8 topics without ayah mappings** (added in MFD-08)

This report proposes approximately 180-220 new topics to achieve balanced coverage, prioritized by domain need and conceptual importance.

## Conclusion

The taxonomy requires approximately 205 additional topics to achieve balanced coverage across all 20 domains and 103 dimensions. Priority must be given to:

1. **Filling critical gaps** - 18 dimensions with 0 topics
2. **Addressing severe underrepresentation** - 48 dimensions with 1 topic
3. **Adding ayah mappings** - 8 topics without mappings

Once these additions are made, the taxonomy will provide comprehensive coverage of Islamic knowledge spanning theology, ethics, law, society, science, arts, and contemporary issues, fulfilling its mission as a complete Quranic thematic framework.
