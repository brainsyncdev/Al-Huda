# MFD-09 Dimension Coverage Audit Report

**Audit Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Auditor:** Taxonomy & Curated Topic Architect  
**Scope:** All 103 dimensions across 20 domains

---

## Executive Summary

The taxonomy contains 103 dimensions with significant coverage imbalances. Only 25 dimensions have subdimensions defined, leaving 78 dimensions without subdimensional structure. Topic distribution is heavily skewed toward core theological and ethical dimensions, while specialized dimensions in creation, interfaith, language, health, arts, and work domains have minimal or zero topic coverage.

**Coverage Statistics:**
- Dimensions with 5+ topics: 12 (11.7%)
- Dimensions with 3-4 topics: 21 (20.4%)
- Dimensions with 2 topics: 22 (21.4%)
- Dimensions with 1 topic: 48 (46.6%)
- Dimensions with 0 topics: 0 (0%) - all have at least 1 topic

**Critical Gaps:** 48 dimensions have only 1 topic, indicating severe underrepresentation.
